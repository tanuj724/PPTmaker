# PPTmaker Design System — Anti-AI-Slop Manifesto & Reference

> **Purpose**: This document is the single source of truth for what makes a PPTmaker deck look like it was designed by a human and not generated by a template factory. It consolidates the anti-patterns, the layout blueprints, the typographic rules, and the quality gates into one reference that every agent can consult.

---

## 1. The Anti-AI-Slop Manifesto

### What is AI slop?

AI slop is the visual and textual equivalent of a stock photo: technically competent, emotionally vacant, and instantly forgettable. It is the result of choosing the path of least resistance at every decision point — the default font, the default layout, the default colour, the default phrasing.

### The 12 Deadly Sins of AI-Generated Decks

| # | Sin | Symptom | Cure |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Template Monotony** | Every slide has the same layout (text left, image right) | Assign ≥ 6 distinct layouts; never repeat consecutively |
| 2 | **Label Titles** | "Overview", "Conclusion", "Statistics" — no claim, no tension | Write assertions: "Nine Crowns, Two Blueprints" |
| 3 | **Floating Bullets** | Bare noun phrases with no lead-in or payload | Every bullet: **bold lead-in + concrete fact** |
| 4 | **Vague Intensifiers** | "amazing", "incredible", "significant" without a number | Replace with a number or delete |
| 5 | **Banned Lexicon** | "delve", "rich tapestry", "testament to", "paves the way" | Hard block — zero tolerance (see content_agent.md §6) |
| 6 | **Pure Black/White** | `#000000` text on `#FFFFFF` canvas | Warm charcoal on soft cream/ice; contrast ≥ 7:1 |
| 7 | **Clipart & Icons-in-Circles** | Generic vector icons for every idea | Authentic editorial photography with framing |
| 8 | **Stretched Images** | Photos warped to fill a frame | Cover-crop to exact aspect; never stretch |
| 9 | **Colliding Text Boxes** | Overlapping shapes, text clipping | Zero-margin frames; explicit bounding boxes; audit harness |
| 10 | **Missing Speaker Notes** | Blank notes or "This slide shows…" | 3-beat script: Transition → Elaboration → Bridge |
| 11 | **No Counter-Evidence** | One-sided advocacy that reads as propaganda | Include ≥ 2 counter-facts per deck |
| 12 | **No Specificity** | Sentences that could apply to any topic | Specificity test: could this appear in a different deck? |

### The Specificity Test (apply to every sentence)

**Ask**: *"Could this sentence appear in a deck about a different subject?"*
- If yes → rewrite with a name, number, date, or place.
- If no → ship it.

```
❌ "Both players achieved remarkable success throughout their careers."
   (True of almost any two athletes. Says nothing.)

✅ "Between them: thirteen Ballon d'Ors, nine European Cups, and 1,750 senior goals."
   (Only true of these two. Carries the argument.)
```

### The "So What?" Test (apply to every slide)

**Ask**: *"So what?"* after reading the slide.
- If the slide doesn't answer → it's trivia. Cut it or reframe around consequence.
- If it does → ship it.

### Contrast Audit (mandatory)

| Pair | Ratio | Grade | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `text_ink` on `canvas` | ≥ 7:1 | AAA | Body copy |
| `accent_metal_light` on `primary_dark` | ≥ 7:1 | AAA | Stat card text |
| `text_muted` on `canvas` | ≥ 4.5:1 | AA-large | Footers, captions only |
| `entity_a` on `canvas` | < 3:1 | FAIL | **Never** body text — structure only |

**Rule**: saturated brand accents are for *structure* (bars, borders, banners, badges) and for *large numbers on dark panels*. Body copy is always `text_ink` on `canvas`.

## 3. Typographic Scale & Font Pairing

### Typeface Voice

| Voice | Title Face | Body Face | Reads As |
|---|---|---|---|
| Editorial heritage | `Georgia` | `Arial` | Weight, tradition, long-form authority |
| Refined classic | `Palatino Linotype` | `Arial` | Prestige, finance, academia |
| Modern technical | `Trebuchet MS` | `Calibri` | Precision, engineering, software |
| Clean corporate | `Segoe UI` (Bold) | `Segoe UI` | Neutral, enterprise, product |
| Minimal studio | `Calibri` (Bold) | `Calibri` | Contemporary, startup, minimal |

**Only use fonts guaranteed present on Windows and macOS.** Never specify a web font — it will silently substitute and wreck the layout.

### Type Scale (16:9, 13.333" × 7.5")

| Element | Heritage | Technical | Minimal | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hero title | 56 pt | 54 pt | 54 pt | Bold, over image — needs overlay for contrast |
| Slide title | 28 pt | 28 pt | 30 pt | Bold, one line, ≤ 9 words |
| Kicker | 11 pt | 10 pt | 10 pt | UPPERCASE, bold, accent metal |
| Section header | 40 pt | 38 pt | 40 pt | Divider slides |
| Big stat number | 38 pt | 36 pt | 40 pt | Bold, title face, fits 1.55" wide |
| Pillar title | 14.5 pt | 14 pt | 15 pt | Bold, ≤ 18 chars/line |
| Bullet lead-in | 16.5 pt | 16 pt | 17 pt | Bold, ink |
| Bullet body | 16.5 pt | 16 pt | 17 pt | Regular, ink |
| Dense bullet (narrow frame) | 12 pt | 12 pt | 12.5 pt | Minimum for card layouts |
| Stat headline | 14 pt | 14 pt | 15 pt | Bold, metal-light on dark |
| Stat subtitle | 9.5 pt | 9.5 pt | 10 pt | Muted-light on dark |
| Table body | 10.5–12 pt | 10.5 pt | 11 pt | Never below 10 |
| Caption / image label | 9 pt | 9 pt | 9 pt | Bold, accent, UPPERCASE |
| Footer / page num | 8.5 pt | 8 pt | 8.5 pt | Muted |

**Absolute floors**: body 12pt · table 10pt · footer 8pt. Below these, shorten the copy instead.

### Typographic Craft Rules

- **Scale ratio**: hero title ≥ 3× body size. This contrast is what makes a deck look designed rather than generated.
- **Weight contrast**: pair bold titles with regular body. Never bold everything — then nothing is emphasised.
- **Line height**: bullets `1.08–1.12`; quote text `1.08`; stat subtitles `1.05`; dense card copy `1.06`.
- **One title face, one body face.** A third face is a mistake.
- **Numerals**: use the title face (serif) for big display numbers — it gives them gravitas. Body numerals stay in the sans.

## 4. The 12 Layout Blueprints

Full coordinate specifications in `knowledge_base/layout_templates_catalog.md`. Summary here.

| # | Layout | Cognitive Job | Density | Best When… |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `hero_cover` | Awe, arrival | Very low | Opening slide only |
| 2 | `asymmetric_editorial_split` | Orient, explain | Medium | 4–5 bullets + one supporting image |
| 3 | `horizontal_timeline_ribbon` | Show progression | Low-medium | 4 chronological milestones |
| 4 | `versus_duel_split` | Contrast two entities | High | Direct A-vs-B comparison |
| 5 | `stat_grid_quad` | Land big numbers | Low reading, high impact | 4 headline metrics |
| 6 | `three_pillar_cards` | Categorise | Medium | Exactly 3 parallel themes |
| 7 | `spotlight_quote_manifesto` | Emotional pause | Very low | One verbatim quote + portrait |
| 8 | `matrix_data_table` | Prove with data | Very high | 5–6 metric rows, head-to-head |
| 9 | `key_takeaways_mosaic` | Synthesise | Medium | 2 conclusions + 1 synthesis |
| 10 | `section_header_divider` | Mark a chapter break | Very low | Only in decks ≥ 14 slides |
| 11 | `dual_stat_showcase` | Two dominant figures | Low | Exactly 2 competing numbers |
| 12 | `hero_closing_climax` | Resolve, memorable exit | Very low | Final slide only |

### Anti-Monotony Allocation Rules

1. **Zero consecutive duplicates.** Slide *n* and slide *n+1* must differ.
2. **≥ 6 distinct layouts** in an 11-slide deck.
3. **Any layout used twice must be separated by ≥ 3 slides** and must carry genuinely different content structure.
4. **Exactly 2 full-bleed hero slides** — opening and closing. A third is filler.
5. **Density must oscillate.** Never two `very high` slides adjacent. After every high-density slide, place a lower-density one.
6. **`section_header_divider` only in long decks** (≥ 14 slides). In an 11-slide deck it wastes a slot.
7. **`three_pillar_cards` only when there are exactly 3 real pillars.** Forcing a fourth or dropping to two breaks the form.
8. **`matrix_data_table` at most once per deck.** Two tables read as a spreadsheet, not a presentation.

### Reference Allocation for an 11-Slide Deck

```
01  hero_cover                   very low   ▁  arrival
02  asymmetric_editorial_split   medium     ▃  orientation
03  horizontal_timeline_ribbon   low-med    ▂  motion, easy
04  versus_duel_split            HIGH       ▆  tension
05  stat_grid_quad               low-read   ▂  punch, release
06  three_pillar_cards           medium     ▃  structure, calm
07  spotlight_quote_manifesto    very low   ▁  emotional breather
08  matrix_data_table            VERY HIGH  █  proof peak
09  asymmetric_editorial_split   medium     ▃  wind-down  (≥3 slides from 02 ✓)
10  key_takeaways_mosaic         medium     ▃  synthesis
11  hero_closing_climax          very low   ▁  resolution
```
Distinct layouts: **9**. Consecutive duplicates: **0**. Density oscillates: **✓**

## 5. Spatial System & Grid

### The Canvas

`13.333" × 7.500"` (16:9). All coordinates in inches.

### Fixed Structural Elements (consistent across every content slide)

```
Left accent stripe      x 0.00 → 0.18    full height     (single-colour) or 0.00→0.09 + 0.09→0.18 (dual-entity)
Header zone             y 0.42 → 1.86    kicker + title + rule
  Kicker                x 0.55, y 0.42, w 9.00, h 0.32     11pt bold metal, UPPERCASE
  Title                 x 0.55, y 0.78, w 10.50, h 0.70    28pt bold title-face primary
  Accent rule           x 0.60, y 1.77, w 2.20, h 0.055    metal
  Hairline rule         x 0.60, y 1.86, w 12.10, h 0.014   rule_hairline
Slide number badge      x 12.30, y 0.50, w 0.58, h 0.58    oval, metal fill, primary numeral
Footer zone             y 7.08 → 7.37
  Footer text           x 0.60, y 7.12, w 7.50, h 0.25     8.5pt bold muted
  Page number           x 11.55, y 7.08, w 1.45, h 0.30    11pt muted, right-aligned
```

### Content Zone (the live area)

```
top    = 2.00"     (minimum; 1.95" for image panels only)
bottom = 6.95"     (maximum — footer starts at 7.08")
left   = 0.60"
right  = 12.733"
usable width  = 12.133"
usable height = 4.95"
```

### The Column Grid

| Split | Columns | Gutter |
|---|---|---|
| 60/40 (asymmetric) | 7.00" + 4.40" | 0.73" |
| 50/50 (versus) | 5.85" + 5.85" | 0.43" |
| 3-up (pillars) | 3.80" × 3 | 0.36" × 2 |
| 4-up (timeline / grid) | 2.85" × 4 or 5.85" × 2 rows | 0.24–0.44" |
| Table + callout | 8.20" + 3.63" | 0.30" |

### Vertical Separation Law

Every distinct semantic block gets **≥ 0.20"** breathing space. Cards in a grid get **≥ 0.30"** gutters. Cramped is the single most common tell of machine-made slides.

| 3.36" | 12 pt | ~40 | 5 | ~200 |
| 2.98" | 10 pt | ~43 | 3 | ~129 |
| 2.50" | 9.5 pt | ~38 | 4 | ~152 |
| 10.933" | 56 pt | ~17 | 1 | ~17 (hero titles!) |

## 7. Image Treatment Rules

| Treatment | Applied to | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| **Dark overlay blend** | Hero cover & closing only | 45–52% blend with `primary_dark`; guarantees AAA text contrast |
| **Plain polish** | All content-slide photos | Contrast ×1.06, Saturation ×1.08; no overlay |
| **Framed panel** | Every content-slide photo | `secondary_dark` backing + 0.08" inset + 1.2pt `accent_metal` border |
| **Caption label** | Above framed panels | 9pt bold metal UPPERCASE, y = panel_top − 0.21 |
| **Duotone wash** *(optional)* | Section dividers | Blend entity accent at 18–25% for mood |

**Never**: stretch to fit, apply drop shadows, use rounded corners on photos, add bevels, or place text directly on an untreated photo.

## 8. The Quality Gates (summary)

Every gate produces a numeric result, not a vibe. See `orchestrator_agent.md` §4 for full rubrics.

| Gate | Check | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| **Gate 1: Evidence** | Verified quantitative facts | ≥ 12 across the dossier |
| | Facts with ≥ 2 independent sources | 100% of headline statistics |
| | Counter-evidence items captured | ≥ 2 |
| **Gate 2: Story** | Controlling argument traceable to slides | ≥ 80% |
| | Slides with distinct `job_to_be_done` | 100% |
| **Gate 3: Copy** | Bullet count per slide | 4–5 (hero/quote exempt) |
| | Bold lead-in compliance | 100% |
| | Banned lexicon hits | 0 |
| | Speaker notes per slide | 3 beats, ≥ 120 words |
| **Gate 4: Layout** | Distinct layout archetypes used | ≥ 6 across 11 slides |
| | Consecutive identical layouts | 0 |
| | Density oscillates | ≥ 2 low-density breathers, ≥ 2 high-density analytical |
| **Gate 5: Images** | Required slots filled | 100% |
| | Source image min dimension | ≥ 1200px on long edge |
| | Distorted / stretched aspect ratios | 0 |
| | Colour-graded to match palette | 100% of hero images |
| **Gate 6: Assembly** | File compiles and re-opens | Required |
| | Slide size | 13.333" × 7.500" (16:9) |
| | Shape/text overlap | 0 collisions |
| | Speaker notes present | 100% of slides |
| | Font sizes at or above minimum | body ≥ 12pt, labels ≥ 8pt |
| | Colour contrast of body text | ≥ 7:1 |

**Fail action**: fix the specific failure, re-run, re-audit. Never ship a deck that fails any gate.

## 9. The Banned Lexicon (hard block — zero tolerance)

`delve` · `rich tapestry` · `testament to` · `beacon of` · `in today's fast-paced` · `ever-evolving` · `ever-changing` · `game-changer` · `game changer` · `paves the way` · `unlock the power of` · `seamlessly` · `seamless` · `robust` · `holistic` · `synergy` · `leverage` (as prose verb) · `landscape of` · `embark on a journey` · `at the end of the day` · `needle-mover` · `it is important to note` · `it should be noted` · `when it comes to` · `in the realm of` · `a myriad of` · `plethora` · `myriad` · `underscore(s)` · `highlight(s) the importance of` · `showcase` · `elevate your` · `take … to the next level` · `revolutionize` · `trailblazing` · `groundbreaking` (without evidence) · `world-class` · `cutting-edge` · `state-of-the-art`

### Substitution Table

| ❌ AI slop | ✅ Editorial replacement |
|---|---|
| "Delve into the rich tapestry of his career" | "Traces a path from Rosario to Barcelona's academy at thirteen" |
| "A testament to human resilience" | "Returned within four months of heart surgery at fifteen" |
| "A game-changer in the landscape of football" | "Redefined what a full-back was expected to contribute" |
| "In today's fast-paced, ever-evolving world…" | *(delete — start with the fact)* |
| "Showcases his incredible talent" | "375 career assists, the highest recorded total" |
| "It is important to remember that…" | *(delete — state it directly)* |
| "Paved the way for future generations" | "Sixteen of the current La Liga squad came through the academy he funded" |
| "An amazing achievement" | "The first player to score in five separate World Cups" |
| "Leveraged his platform" | "Used his 500M-follower reach to fund…" |
| "Highlights the importance of discipline" | "Trained the same six-day schedule for nineteen seasons" |

## 10. Final Delivery Format

When the deck ships, report to the user:

1. **Working title & controlling argument** (one sentence each)
2. **Slide count, aspect ratio, palette name**
3. **Layout allocation table** — proving diversity (slide → archetype)
4. **Asset integrity** — N images embedded, sources, any fallbacks used
5. **Slide-by-slide outline** with each slide's job-to-be-done
6. **Gate scores** — 1 line per gate, pass/fail
7. **File locations** — `.pptx` + reusable build script
8. **What you'd change with more time** (a human always says this)

## 11. The Human Test

Before shipping, ask:

- **Would a human designer have made this?** If the answer is "no, they would have varied it more" — vary it more.
- **Would a human editor have cut this slide?** If the answer is "yes, it doesn't advance the argument" — cut it.
- **Would a human copywriter have written this sentence?** If the answer is "no, they would have been more specific" — be more specific.
- **Would a human photo editor have shipped this image?** If the answer is "no, they would have found something better" — find something better.

**The goal is not perfection. The goal is to feel like a person made it.**
