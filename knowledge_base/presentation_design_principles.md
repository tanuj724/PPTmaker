# Presentation Design & Creative Intelligence Knowledge Base

> **Core Philosophy**: A world-class presentation is not a repeated form template with swapped text. It is an editorial journey. A creative human designer approaches each slide like a magazine spread or an exhibition hall: varying density, alternating visual weight, matching layout geometry to the emotional and intellectual purpose of the content, and never presenting two identical visual layouts back-to-back.

---

## 1. The 7 Laws of Human-Calibrated Presentation Design

### Law 1: The Anti-Monotony Rule (The 80/20 Layout Variation Law)
- **Problem**: Repetitive slides (e.g., 10 consecutive slides with bullet points on the left and an image on the right) cause cognitive fatigue within 90 seconds.
- **Solution**: No two consecutive slides may share the same structural layout. If Slide 2 is an *Asymmetric Split*, Slide 3 must be a *Timeline Progression*, Slide 4 a *Direct Versus Duel Split*, Slide 5 a *4-Card Metric Grid*, Slide 6 a *3-Pillar Card Matrix*, etc.

### Law 2: Form Follows Cognitive Function
Layouts must be chosen based on what the information *is*, not just to fill space:
- **Comparison / Rivalry** $\rightarrow$ `versus_duel_split` (Side-by-side opposing colored columns)
- **Chronology / Milestones** $\rightarrow$ `horizontal_timeline_ribbon` (Sequential connected milestone nodes)
- **Key Quantitative Metrics** $\rightarrow$ `stat_grid_quad` or `dual_stat_showcase` (Oversized numbers with context cards)
- **Categorical Frameworks** $\rightarrow$ `three_pillar_cards` (3 vertical thematic pillars)
- **Key Quotation / Philosophy** $\rightarrow$ `spotlight_quote_manifesto` (Bold quotation with focal imagery)
- **Detailed Data / Records** $\rightarrow$ `matrix_data_table` (Structured row-banded comparison grid)
- **Deep Narrative Context** $\rightarrow$ `asymmetric_editorial_split` (60/40 hero narrative + stat badge)
- **Chapter Transition** $\rightarrow$ `section_header_divider` (Dramatic full-bleed milestone pause)

### Law 3: Dynamic Visual Rhythm & Pacing
A human presentation breathes through intentional tempo changes:
```
[Slide 1: Cinematic Cover]       -> High Impact, Expansive, Minimal Text
       |
[Slide 2: Overview / Hook]        -> Dense Context, 2-Card Asymmetric Split
       |
[Slide 3: Timeline Evolution]     -> Horizontal Motion, 4-Node Sequence
       |
[Slide 4: Deep Duel Comparison]   -> Symmetrical Tension, Dual Color Split
       |
[Slide 5: Big Numbers Grid]       -> Punchy, High Visual Contrast, 4 Stat Cards
       |
[Slide 6: 3 Pillars Strategy]     -> Categorical Triad, Structured Columns
       |
[Slide 7: Spotlight / Quote]      -> Emotional Climax, Breathable Negative Space
       |
[Slide 8: Data Matrix Table]      -> High Information Density, Analytical
       |
[Slide 9: Asymmetric Narrative]   -> Storytelling Balance, Editorial Media Frame
       |
[Slide 10: Key Takeaway Cards]    -> Synthesis, 3-Card Conclusion Horizon
       |
[Slide 11: Cinematic Climax]      -> Full-Bleed Resolution, Lasting Impression
```

### Law 4: Typographic Contrast & Emotional Scale
- **Display Typography**: Serifs (`Georgia`, `Palatino Linotype`, `Baskerville`) for gravitas, prestige, and historical editorial authority; Clean Geometric Sans (`Trebuchet MS`, `Segoe UI`, `Calibri`) for tech, speed, and modern precision.
- **Size Contrast Ratio**: The Hero Title (54–58pt) must be at least $3\times$ the size of Body Text (16–17pt). Section Kickers (10–11pt) should be uppercase and letter-spaced to serve as visual grounding anchors.
- **Two-Tone Lead-Ins**: Never drop a plain wall of text. Every bullet point begins with a bold 2–4 word actionable lead-in in high-contrast ink, followed by concrete quantitative evidence.

### Law 5: Intentional Color Harmony & Semantic Accents
- **Primary Canvas**: Never blinding `#FFFFFF` or flat `#000000`. Use warm luxury cream (`#F9F4EB`), crisp ice slate (`#F7F9FC`), or midnight obsidian (`#0E1626`).
- **Semantic Accents**: In comparative or narrative decks, assign specific colors to distinct entities (e.g., Messi Albiceleste Cyan `#00A8E8` vs Ronaldo CR7 Crimson `#E63946`, united by Championship Gold `#D4AF37`).
- **Contrast Compliance**: Ensure all text passes WCAG AAA contrast ratio ($>7:1$).

### Law 6: Negative Space as a Design Element
- White space (negative space) is not empty space; it is visual breathing room.
- Maintain at least `0.40"` to `0.60"` margins on all slide edges.
- Provide `0.20"` to `0.30"` vertical separation between distinct semantic blocks to eliminate crowding and text collisions.

### Law 7: Curated Editorial Photography (No Generic Clipart)
- Use authentic, high-resolution editorial photography that captures action, expression, architecture, or historical artifacts.
- Process images through center-weighted cover crops, subtle contrast enhancement, and matching color-tinted borders.
- Never stretch, squash, or distort asset aspect ratios.
